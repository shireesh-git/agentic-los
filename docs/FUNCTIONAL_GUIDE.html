Corporate lending · Proof of concept

How a corporate loan moves through the agentic LOS

A guide for credit, risk and operations colleagues. It covers each stage from the first prospect conversation to disbursement: who acts, what the AI agents prepare, and what you verify and decide.

The idea The journey Stage by stage Verifying agent work Decisions & controls Who's who Measuring it What is demo Glossary

The idea in one minute

In a conventional LOS, people type data from the borrower's documents into screens, and the credit analysis is built separately. In this LOS, AI agents read the documents and run the credit analysis. They then fill the screens for you, showing where each value came from. Your job moves from keying to checking and deciding.

1

Agents prepare

When an application reaches a preparation step, agents start work at the same time. They read documents, run due-diligence checks and build the credit case (CAM). Then they fill each workspace. Every value carries its source: document · page · confidence.

2

You verify by exception

A verify queue shows what needs your eye. High-confidence values arrive pre-ticked, so you can confirm them in one click. Conflicts, low-confidence values and AI-drafted text are flagged for individual review. Anything can be changed, with a reason.

3

People decide

No agent submits, approves, returns, sanctions or disburses. Each decision belongs to a named person in a named role. A remark is required with every decision, and everything goes into the audit trail.

Stage by stage

For each step: who acts, what the agents have prepared, what you do, and what must be in place before you can move on. SLAs are in calendar days.

1

Prospect Sourcing Agent prepares

Prospect Sourcing Maker (RM) · SLA 5 days
Agents
For a new prospect, the RM uploads the request letter, KYC and audited financials before the borrower exists in the system. The intake agent reads every document in parallel and proposes identity and facility values with page references. It also checks for an existing customer or application with the same PAN, CIN or name. Once the application is created, three lanes run in parallel: identity & duplicate check, automated due-diligence checks and document intake.
You
Review the proposed values and use Accept all high-confidence, editing anything that is wrong with a reason. If a scanned page could not be read, type the value in. Choose the sector. Record the sourcing decision and the prospect discussion. Record the manual checks (CRILC, Central Fraud Registry, CEIB, Enhanced Due Diligence, UDIN, KYC, MLRA).
Gate
Mandatory due-diligence checks (MCA, CRILC, KYC, name screening, MLRA) must be complete. A match or failure on a mandatory check needs a Compliance VP exception.
Actions
Submit to IP Note Defer Drop Withdraw
The agent reports what it can't read; it doesn't guess. If a value sits on a scanned page and no image-reading model is configured, the field is left empty and marked scanned page not read. A borrower created from documents never gets placeholder identifiers.

Arrives pre-ticked when

  • Confidence is 85% or higher, for example when several documents agree or the value matches the record.
  • The critic found no issue with its evidence or figures.
  • The field may be bulk-confirmed.

Even then, nothing is accepted until a person clicks Confirm selected.

Always needs individual review

  • Decision-driving fields: risk grade, system recommendation, internal rating, decision, structure, deviations, KYC status, watch-list check, legal name, requested amount.
  • AI-drafted narrative.
  • Demo values standing in for bank models.
  • Conflicts and low-confidence values.

ActionWhenNeeds
ConfirmThe value is right—

ReopenA decided value needs another look—

The life of a field

Proposed by an agent→ Confirmedor Overridden (with reason)→ Locked against re-runs→ if a later run disagrees → Conflict, for you to resolve
Your corrections flow back into the analysis. If you change a financial (CMA) figure or a collateral value, the credit case re-runs automatically with your figure. The ratios, risk view and CAM then reflect it. In a test, changing revenue from ₹1,050 Cr to ₹1,400 Cr re-ran the case, and the EBITDA margin moved to 3.2%. The engine's own value is kept alongside yours for audit.

Decisions & controls

The workflow decides who may act. People take every decision, and every decision is recorded.

DecisionWho / whereWhat happens
SubmitMakersMoves to the next step once the step's gate is satisfied.
Approve / RecommendCheckers, VPs, EVPMoves forward. The label follows the bank's wording, such as Recommend for sanction or Recommend for disbursement.
ReturnCheckers, VPs, committeesBack to the stage's maker, assigned to the person who last worked it, with your comment shown on their step.
RejectCheckers, committeesEnds the application.
AllocateCRP EVP, CRP VPAssigns the next step to a named person who holds the right role.
SanctionCommitteesRecords the amount, the minutes and a snapshot of the facility.
DeferPS and IP Note makersPuts the application on hold until a date. The SLA is paused and only Reactivate is offered.
Drop / WithdrawMakersCloses the application, for the bank's reason or at the customer's request. A reason is required.

Controls built in

  • A remark on every decision, at every step.
  • Maker–checker throughout; a checker cannot edit the maker's record (reviewers are read-only).
  • Gates before submit: due diligence, IP Note, assessment, consent and CPs, tranche and checklist.
  • Decision fields where the process asks for them, such as the material-information date, significant changes and committee minutes.
  • Compliance VP exception is the only way past a mandatory due-diligence match.
  • Escalate and Reassign on any active application.

What the AI is not allowed to do

  • Take any workflow or credit decision.
  • Invent a figure. Any AI text that quotes a number not in the record is rejected.
  • Accept a value on a person's behalf.
  • Overwrite a value a person has confirmed or changed.
  • Act unseen. Agent work appears in the audit trail like any other action, labelled with the agent's name.

What is demo in this proof of concept

The workflow, the agents, the verification and the controls are real. The bank's own systems and models are not yet connected, and the screens label the stand-ins.

Labelled demo stand-ins

  • Core banking (CIF, limits, CBS security IDs), bureau, MCA and screening services
  • Internal rating scale, PD/LGD, RAROC and the pricing grid
  • The authority matrix (shown; routing follows a fixed chain)
  • Sign-in (a picker, not the bank's identity system)

Outside the LOS

  • Payment execution and reconciliation
  • Reading scanned pages needs an image-capable AI model, or OCR software, to be configured
  • Demo documents: a fictional borrower, Zeta Foods Private Limited. The bank's own document set is the next step.

Glossary

Agent
A piece of software that does a defined job: reading documents, running checks, or filling a workspace from the credit case. It proposes values; it never decides.
AP
Application Processing: the full appraisal and final sanction stage.
CAM
Credit Appraisal Memorandum. Here it is produced by the credit-intelligence engine, which runs the analysis and writes the sections.
CMA
Credit Monitoring Arrangement data: the financial statements and projections in the bank's format.
Confidence
How sure the agent is about a value. It is higher when documents agree with each other or with the record.
Conflict
A new proposal that differs from a value already decided, or documents that disagree with each other.
CP / CS
Conditions precedent (before disbursement) and conditions subsequent (after it, due in 90 days by default).
CRILC
RBI's Central Repository of Information on Large Credits.
CRP / LPF
Credit Risk Policy team / Lending & Project Finance team.
Critic
An automatic check on every agent proposal: is there evidence, and does every quoted figure appear in the source?
DD
Due diligence: the 15 checks (8 automated, 7 recorded by a person).
DIS
Disbursement stage.
ESMS / ESDD
Environmental & Social Management System / Due Diligence.
IP
In-Principle stage: the IP Note, the first credit assessment and the in-principle sanction.
MLRA
Money-laundering risk assessment.
NTB / ETB
New to bank / existing to bank.
Pinned credit case
The specific credit-case run tied to an application, so every step looks at the same analysis until something changes.
POS
Post Sanction stage: consent, documentation and conditions.
Pre-ticked
A high-confidence value selected in advance for one-click confirmation. It still needs a person to confirm it.
PS
Prospect Sourcing stage.
RAROC
Risk-adjusted return on capital.
SLA
Target days for a step. Breaches are flagged on the step, the dashboard and the MIS.
