Close with proof and clear boundaries

State what is verified, what is configurable and what is outside demo scope.

Verified demo scope

  • End-to-end workflow from sourcing through Exit (approved).
  • Persistent multi-party/facility/project records and all governance workspaces.
  • Fifteen due-diligence checks, evidence, comments, exceptions and 90-day controls.
  • Approval, documents, conditions, covenants, disbursement and repeat tranche.
  • Audit events, working-day SLA, MIS and honest adapter states.
  • 437/437 automated tests passed, including the 42-change presentation-readiness contract.

Do not claim as production-ready

  • No live CBS/LMS/bureau/regulatory connections are supplied.
  • Authority matrices, formulas, policy thresholds and legal templates are labelled demo/configurable until the client confirms them.
  • Enterprise IAM, e-signature, antivirus, archival immutability, performance, resilience and disaster recovery remain production work.
  • Exit (approved) does not mean payment execution or settlement.
Closing line: “The functional journey is demo-complete; productionization begins when the bank confirms its authoritative policies, templates, integrations and non-functional requirements.”
